# FR6FX Core XAUUSD Trading Playbook
1. Primary Pair: XAUUSD (Gold) correlated inversely with DXY (US Dollar Index).
2. Killzones: London Session (07:00–10:00 UTC) and New York Session (12:00–15:00 UTC). Never force trades during low-volume late NY or mid-Asia consolidation.
3. Liquidity First: Always identify Asian High/Low, Previous Day High/Low (PDH/PDL), and Equal Highs/Lows (EQH/EQL) before looking for entries.
4. Entry Model: Wait for a sweep of buy-side or sell-side liquidity into a Higher Timeframe (H1/M15) Order Block or Fair Value Gap (FVG), followed by an M5 Change of Character (CHoCH) with displacement.
5. Risk Management: Minimum 1:2.5 Risk-to-Reward. Never enter 15 minutes before or after high-impact USD news (NFP, CPI, FOMC, Fed Chair speech).
